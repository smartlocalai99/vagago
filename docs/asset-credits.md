# Photography credits

The VagaGo brand image comes from the supplied project file `logo.jpeg`. The original is preserved. `assets/brand-logo.jpeg` is an unchanged copy; icon and favicon PNGs are format/size conversions. The header displays the mark using a clipped image view alongside a text wordmark.

Manrope and Cormorant Garamond are bundled through their `@expo-google-fonts` packages; their SIL Open Font License files remain in the installed font packages.

Sources checked 1 November 2026. Images are bundled locally so the preview does not depend on a photo CDN. Downloaded copies were resized to a maximum dimension of 1600 px and recompressed as JPEG; no scene content was generated or altered.

`hillscape.jpg` and `forest.jpg` depict Vagamon according to their photographers' source listings. Property images are **representative stock photographs for fictional preview stays**, not photographs of onboarded hotels or evidence of their amenities. Replace these property images with authorized hotel photographs before publishing actual listings. Do not imply that the photographers or photographed properties endorse Vagago.
